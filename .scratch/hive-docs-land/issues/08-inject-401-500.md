## Question

After merge, `app` injects 401 and 500 onto every public operation.

Use the mechanism [How does utoipa 5 inject 401 and 500 after merge?](02-utoipa-inject-401-500.md) records. Cells keep the statuses they choose. `Problem` stays the one shared component name. `GET /health` stays off the spec.

If that research finds more than one viable mechanism, stop and graduate a grilling ticket. Do not pick.

## Answer

`merge` walks the merged `OpenApi` and `or_insert`s 401 and 500. Each response is `application/problem+json` with `$ref` `#/components/schemas/Problem`. A cell status stays. `GET /health` stays off the spec.
